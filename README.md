# Four planet candidates in 3D

Interactive viewer for the exoplanet candidates in:

- Fraser 2026a, *Three small transiting planet candidates from TESS around TOI-678, GJ 237 and TOI-5997* — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23249265
- Fraser 2026b, *A 4.27-day Neptune-mass planet candidate around the K dwarf HD 297396* — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23249329

Live: https://samsonmaximus.github.io/planets/

Orbits, radii, periods and epochs are the published values. Companion signals (TOI-678.01, TOI-5997 b, TOI-6263.01) use epochs from the NASA Exoplanet Archive TOI table, and their impact parameters (not measured in these papers) are set to 0.3. HD 297396 b does not transit; its inclination is unknown and is drawn at 75°, and its radius comes from a mass–radius relation. Sizes are enlarged unless "True scale" is on. The light curve uses a quadratic limb-darkened overlap model and is illustrative, not the fit.
